Terms of engagement

Bespoke Concept · Last updated: 23 November 2026

These terms govern the Bonomelli Estate Bespoke Concept. This is a translation: the Italian text prevails in case of any difference.

1. Scope

Bonomelli Estate produces for the Client three high-resolution renderings of a Bonomelli Estate concept, set in the space indicated by the Client: two overall views and one detail.

2. Start of the commission

The commission starts once Bonomelli Estate has received the payment, the photographs and the approximate measurements. By paying, the Client accepts these terms.

3. Client materials

The Client supplies photographs and measurements that the Client is entitled to share and warrants that they are accurate. Bonomelli Estate uses them only to carry out the commission.

4. Delivery

The renderings are delivered electronically within three working days of the start of the commission. If the materials received do not allow the work to be done, Bonomelli Estate says so, and the period runs from receipt of complete materials.

5. Revision

One revision of the delivered renderings is included. It must be requested within fourteen days of delivery and is delivered within two working days of the request. The revision covers changes to the same concept. A different concept or further revisions are a new commission.

6. Price and payment

The price is €690, payable in advance through the payment page on the website. The tax document is issued under the applicable regime.

7. Deduction from the quotation

If, within twelve months of delivery, the Client commissions Bonomelli Estate to build the piece, the amount paid is deducted in full from the quotation.

8. Nature of the renderings

The renderings are a work of visual concept design. They illustrate an idea and are not a technical, construction or structural project. Measurements, structures, foundations, services, permits and regulatory compliance must be verified by licensed professionals before any build.

9. Intellectual property

Designs, concepts and renderings remain the property of Bonomelli Estate, including where they are protected by registered designs. The Client receives a personal, non-exclusive licence to assess the piece and show it to the Client’s own advisers. The Client may not have the piece built, in whole or in part, nor pass the renderings to third parties for that purpose, without a written agreement with Bonomelli Estate. Publishing the renderings requires written consent and the credit “Bonomelli Estate”.

10. Confidentiality

Bonomelli Estate does not publish images showing the Client’s property without the Client’s written consent.

11. Cancellation and refunds

If the Client cancels before the commission starts, or if Bonomelli Estate cannot accept it, the amount is refunded in full. After the renderings are delivered, the price is not refundable. A consumer Client who asks for the commission to start immediately acknowledges that the right of withdrawal ends once the service has been fully performed.

12. Personal data

The Client’s data is processed in line with the site’s privacy notice.

13. Law and jurisdiction

Italian law applies. For consumer Clients, the court of their place of residence has jurisdiction; for other Clients, the court of Verona.

14. Contact

Bonomelli Estate, San Bonifacio (VR), Italy · studio@bonomelliestate.it

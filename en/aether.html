Bookcases

AETHER

Three stone bookcases, one family. Travertine structure, sliding tempered-glass doors with bronze frames, warm LED light built into the shelves and plinth, a corner niche with curved shelves. Stone shelves or teak, rosewood or bamboo inserts.

Fluted pilasters with Corinthian capitals, cornice with Greek key and rosettes.

Free-standing Corinthian columns on each side, corner base with a carved Greek key.

Plain pilasters, a fine Greek key and dark stone bases: the same architecture, pared back.

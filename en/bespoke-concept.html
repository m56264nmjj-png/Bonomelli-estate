How it works

The three working days start once the payment, the photographs and the measurements have arrived.

  1. I

    Payment

    You complete the payment on the secure page and accept the terms of engagement.

  2. II

    Photos and measurements

    You send photographs of the place, approximate measurements and your chosen piece to studio@bonomelliestate.it.

  3. III

    The three renderings

    Within three working days you receive the three renderings of the piece in your space.

  4. IV

    The revision

    You may ask for one revision, delivered within two working days.
